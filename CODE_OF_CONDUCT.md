# Code of Conduct

LureRadar adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, focusing on what is best for
the community.

Not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit
permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

Specific to this project: do not post live malicious links (defang them), do
not share victims' personal data, and do not use project spaces to promote or
sell anything related to the scams we track.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting on this repository (choose "Report a vulnerability"
and mark the report as a conduct issue). Maintainers will review promptly and
fairly, respect the reporter's privacy, and apply the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
