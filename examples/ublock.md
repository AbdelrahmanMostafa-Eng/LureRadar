# uBlock Origin

Dashboard → **Filter lists** → scroll to **Import...** under *Custom*, paste:

```text
https://lureradar.vercel.app/feeds/adguard.txt
```

then click **Apply changes**. uBlock Origin shows its strict-blocking page when
you navigate to a listed domain, with an option to proceed if you are sure.

One-click subscribe link (paste into the address bar while uBlock Origin is installed):

```text
abp:subscribe?location=https%3A%2F%2Flureradar.vercel.app%2Ffeeds%2Fadguard.txt&title=LureRadar%20launch%20lures
```
